Our Leadership

Meet Our Founder & Managing Director.

Sam Naragala

Founder & Managing Director

Sam Naragala is an award-winning finance professional, entrepreneur and trusted adviser with more than a decade of experience across accounting, taxation, strategic business advisory and financial services.

Professional Qualifications & Client Advisory

As a Chartered Accountant (CA), Chartered Tax Adviser (CTA), Registered Tax Agent and qualified Finance and Mortgage Broker, Sam supports individuals, entrepreneurs and established businesses across a diverse range of industries. He is known for bringing clarity to complex financial and taxation matters, identifying meaningful opportunities for growth, and helping clients build strategies for sustainable, long-term wealth creation.

Awards & Recognition

Sam’s contribution to the profession has been recognised nationally. In 2025, he was named Young Chartered Accountant of the Year (Tasmania) by Chartered Accountants Australia and New Zealand (CA ANZ) and received the Mentor of the Year award at the Australian Accounting Awards, recognitions that reflect both his professional expertise and his generous commitment to developing others.

Professional Leadership

Beyond his professional practice, Sam actively contributes to the advancement of the accounting profession. He serves as a Regional Councillor for Chartered Accountants Australia and New Zealand (CA ANZ) and as a member of La Trobe University's Bachelor and Master of Accounting Course Advisory Committee, helping strengthen the connection between education, industry and the next generation of finance professionals.

Business Experience & Advisory Philosophy

As a business owner and entrepreneur himself, Sam understands the ambition, discipline and commercial judgement required to build and sustain a successful business. This firsthand experience shapes his advisory philosophy, combining technical expertise with practical business insight and a genuine understanding of his clients' aspirations.

Commitment to Clients

For Sam, the true measure of professional excellence lies not merely in the quality of advice delivered, but in the enduring value it creates, the confidence it inspires and the opportunities it unlocks.
